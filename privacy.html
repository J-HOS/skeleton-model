Privacy Policy

My Anatomy (the Skeleton Model project) - last updated November 14, 2026

My Anatomy is a free anatomy study app. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not collect personal information.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves a few settings in your browser's local storage on this device: the structures you viewed most recently, your best study scores (numbers only), whether you have seen the first-run notice, and the "Both sides" setting. Your typed answers are not saved. None of this leaves your device.

To delete it, open Menu > Credits & licenses > Clear stored data, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

What the website host sees

The app and its 3D model are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub keeps standard logs of requests, including your IP address, for security and operation. We do not receive or use these logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

What we do not do

We do not sell, share or transfer data, because we do not collect any. The app does not use cookies, location, camera, microphone or contacts.

Children

The app is an educational reference and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version stores or sends anything new, this page will be updated before that version is released.
